THE DECISION CONTRACT

Explicit questions. Typed outputs.

The same schema is sent for every ticket. The expected sample answers are never sent to Laya.

Three questions, one model call

choice → department
A label from Billing, Technical, Sales or Other, with its probability distribution.
score → urgency
An expected ordinal value from 0 to 2. The displayed level is the most likely level, not the rounded average.
noul → cancellation
The model's estimated probability that the customer expresses intent to cancel.

Our review policy

Suggest human review if any confidence is below the slider threshold, cancellation probability is at least 50%, or expected urgency is at least 1.5.

Confidence is a separate model output. It is not measured accuracy. This PoC does not fit temperatures or validate a production threshold.
